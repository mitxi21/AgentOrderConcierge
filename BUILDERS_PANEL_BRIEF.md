# Builders Panel — Brief (Salesforce, FDE role)

Source: `Builders_Panel_.pdf` (Salesforce recruitment deck). Demo date: **2026-09-23**.

## Format

Final step of the recruitment process: a **45-minute presentation** introducing yourself as a
candidate and demoing an agent you built.

| # | Chapter | Time |
|---|---------|------|
| 1 | Introduction — brief introduction of yourself | 5 min |
| 2 | Asset — describe and show a working AI agent you built | 30 min |
| 3 | Why me — what makes you the ideal Forward Deployment Engineer | 10 min |
| 4 | Q&A | 10 min |

## Chapter 2 — the four required areas

**The Agent (what it is)**
Purpose, who the user is, what job-to-be-done it performs. What outcome a successful run
produces, and how that outcome is measured.

**AI Tooling (how it is built)**
Which model, tools/MCPs, data sources, memory, and orchestration layer were chosen. The
rationale behind each choice and how the pieces fit together end-to-end.

**Boundaries & Guardrails (where it must not go)**
What is in and out of scope. Safety, policy, and data-access controls. Where a human stays in
the loop, and what triggers an escalation or a refusal.

**Issues & Trade-offs (what you learned the hard way)**
Failure modes encountered, trade-offs made (cost, latency, accuracy, autonomy), and how they
were detected, fixed, or mitigated. What you would change with more time.

## Evaluation criteria — elements that should transpire in the talk track

- **Agent Impact** — significance of the problem the agent addresses and the outcome it moves.
- **Individual Technical Contribution** — clarity of hands-on work: prompts, tools,
  integrations, evals, and code personally owned.
- **Strategic Alignment** — understanding of the user, the job-to-be-done, and the success
  metric the agent was built to serve.
- **Business Fit** — how the agent slots into the surrounding business process: what it
  replaces, accelerates, or unblocks.
- **Technical Vision** — rationale for the chosen model, tools/MCPs, data, memory, and
  orchestration, and why over the alternatives.
- **Boundaries & Guardrails** — clarity of scope, safety controls, data access limits,
  human-in-the-loop points, and escalation paths.
- **Reliability & Evaluation** — awareness of failure modes, trade-offs (cost, latency,
  accuracy, autonomy), and how the agent is tested, monitored, and improved.
- **Explanation & Justification** — clarity and reasoning behind design choices, refusals, and
  open issues.
- **Soft Skills & Time Management** — communication, presence, and ability to structure the
  narrative within the allocated time.

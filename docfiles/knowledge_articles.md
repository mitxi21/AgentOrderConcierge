# Knowledge Articles — Order & Case Concierge

Paste-ready content for 5 Knowledge articles, plus a scripted 6th (the Phase 10 workflow diagram). Written to satisfy your eval
cases directly: `happy_policy_faq` needs "day"/"return"/"policy" in the
retrieved answer, `happy_policy_faq_shipping` needs "business day"/"shipping" —
both are covered by the concrete numbers below, not vague filler.

Each article is short on purpose — the agent is instructed to answer in 2-3
sentences, so a bloated source article doesn't help retrieval quality, it just
makes the vector chunks noisier.

---

## Article 1: Return Policy

**Title:** Return Policy

**Summary:** How long customers have to return an item and what condition it must be in.

**Body:**
```
Most items can be returned within 30 days of the delivery date for a full
refund to the original payment method. Items must be unused, in their
original packaging, and include all accessories. Final-sale and clearance
items are not eligible for return. To start a return, contact support with
your order number — a team member will provide a prepaid return label and
process the refund once the item is received and inspected.
```

---

## Article 2: Standard Shipping Timelines

**Title:** Standard Shipping Timelines

**Summary:** How long orders take to ship and arrive under standard and expedited shipping.

**Body:**
```
Orders are processed within 1 business day of purchase. Standard shipping
typically takes 5-7 business days from the ship date; expedited shipping
takes 2-3 business days. Delivery times may run a day or two longer for
remote areas. Once an order ships, a tracking number is sent by email.
```

---

## Article 3: Warranty Terms

**Title:** Warranty Terms

**Summary:** What the standard manufacturer warranty covers and how to file a claim.

**Body:**
```
All products include a 1-year manufacturer warranty covering defects in
materials and workmanship. The warranty does not cover accidental damage,
misuse, or normal wear and tear. To file a warranty claim, contact support
with your order number and a description of the issue; approved claims are
resolved with a repair, replacement, or store credit at our discretion.
```

---

## Article 4: Exchange Process

**Title:** Exchange Process

**Summary:** How to exchange an item for a different size, color, or model within the return window.

**Body:**
```
Items can be exchanged for a different size, color, or model within the
same 30-day window as our return policy, as long as the item is unused and
in its original packaging. There is no restocking fee for exchanges.
Contact support with your order number to start an exchange; once the
original item is received, the replacement ships within 1-2 business days.
```

---

## Article 5: Damaged or Wrong Item Received

**Title:** Damaged or Wrong Item Received

**Summary:** What to do if an order arrives damaged or the wrong item was shipped.

**Body:**
```
If an order arrives damaged or you received the wrong item, report it
within 14 days of delivery. Include a photo of the item and its packaging
when you contact support with your order number. There is no charge for
return shipping in these cases, and you can choose a replacement or a full
refund once the issue is confirmed.
```

## Setup steps (Phase 2 of your runbook)

1. **Confirm Salesforce Knowledge is enabled.** Setup → Knowledge Settings.
   If this is the first time it's being turned on, Salesforce will prompt a
   one-time setup wizard (default article record type, default data
   category group if you have one — you can skip data categories entirely
   for this scope).

2. **Create the 5 articles.** App Launcher → Knowledge (or the Knowledge tab
   if it's on your nav bar) → New. For each article: paste the Title, use
   the Summary field if your article layout has one, and paste the Body
   text into the main rich-text content field (commonly labeled "Article
   Body" or similar on the default Lightning Knowledge layout — the exact
   field label depends on your org's article type/layout, but there will be
   one primary rich-text area).

3. **Publish each one — don't leave them as Draft.** After saving, use the
   Publish action on each article. Draft articles are not retrievable by
   Data Cloud/Agentforce indexing, so this step is easy to forget and will
   silently break retrieval later.

4. **Bring them into Data Cloud.** Per your runbook, the current
   recommended path is an **Agentforce Data Library** pointed at your
   Knowledge articles, rather than wiring a raw Data Cloud stream + search
   index by hand — the Data Library handles ingestion, chunking, and vector
   indexing for you. In Setup, search for "Data Library" (or find it inside
   Agent Builder's data/knowledge configuration) and point it at your
   Knowledge object, filtered to published articles. Verify the exact menu
   path in Salesforce Help before you start, since this UI has shifted
   between releases.

5. **Test retrieval standalone before wiring it to the agent.** Most Data
   Library UIs let you run a test query directly. Try "what's your return
   policy" and "how long does shipping take" and confirm each pulls back
   the right article — debugging retrieval quality through the live agent
   conversation is much slower than checking it here first.

6. **Only after that's confirmed**, wire the Data Library as the retrieval
   source for your Policy/FAQ topic's Knowledge Search action in Agent
   Builder (Phase 4 in your runbook).

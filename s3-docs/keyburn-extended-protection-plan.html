Keyburn Extended Protection Plan: Terms

Document owner: Keyburn Legal. Version 2.0, effective 1 July 2026.

1. What the plan is

The Keyburn Extended Protection Plan is an optional paid plan. It adds 2 years of cover after the standard 1-year manufacturer warranty ends, so a covered product is protected for 3 years in total from the delivery date.

2. When it can be bought

The plan can be added at checkout or up to 60 days after the delivery date. After 60 days it can no longer be added to that order.

3. What it covers

  • Defects in materials and workmanship during the 2 extended years, on the same terms as the manufacturer warranty.
  • Accidental damage, such as drops and spills, from the delivery date. The plan covers one accidental damage claim in any 12-month period.

The plan does not cover loss, theft, cosmetic damage that does not affect how the product works, or damage caused on purpose.

4. Making a claim

Claims are made through Keyburn customer support with the order number. Approved claims are resolved by repair or, when repair is not possible, by replacement with the same or an equivalent model. The plan never pays out cash.

5. Cancelling the plan

The plan can be cancelled for a full refund within 30 days of purchase if no claim has been made.

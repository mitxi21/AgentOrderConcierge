Keyburn Delivery Service Levels

Document owner: Keyburn Logistics. Version 3.2, effective 1 September 2026. Applies to all orders shipped from the Keyburn warehouse.

1. Carriers

Keyburn ships standard orders with its national parcel carrier and expedited orders with its express courier partner. The carrier is chosen automatically by the warehouse at dispatch; customers cannot choose the carrier.

2. Delivery attempts

The carrier makes up to three delivery attempts on consecutive business days. If nobody is available to receive the parcel after the third attempt, the parcel is taken to the nearest carrier pickup point.

Parcels are held at the pickup point for 7 calendar days. Parcels that are not collected within those 7 days are returned to the Keyburn warehouse, and the order is refunded minus the original shipping charge.

3. Late or missing parcels

A parcel is considered delayed when its tracking has shown no new scan for 5 business days. At that point Keyburn opens a carrier investigation on the customer's behalf. The customer does not need to contact the carrier.

A carrier investigation is concluded within 10 business days. If the parcel is confirmed lost, the customer chooses between a replacement shipped by expedited delivery at no cost, or a full refund.

4. Signature on delivery

Orders with a value above 500 euros require a signature on delivery and cannot be left with a neighbour or in a safe place.

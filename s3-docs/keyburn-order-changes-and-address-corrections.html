Keyburn Order Changes and Address Corrections

Document owner: Keyburn Customer Operations. Version 1.4, effective 15 August 2026.

1. Changing an order before it ships

While an order is in Draft or Processing, the customer can change the delivery address, the item's size or colour, or the shipping speed, by contacting customer support with the order number. These changes are free of charge.

2. Correcting the address after an order has shipped

Once an order has shipped, its contents can no longer be changed. The delivery address can still be corrected through a carrier redirect, under these conditions:

  • The new address must be in the same country as the original address.
  • The redirect must be requested before the carrier's first delivery attempt.
  • A carrier redirect costs a flat fee of 4.99 euros, charged to the original payment method.

A redirect usually adds 1 to 2 business days to the delivery time.

3. Gift orders

Any order can be marked as a gift before it ships. Gift orders are delivered without prices on the packing slip. A gift message of up to 200 characters can be added at no cost.
